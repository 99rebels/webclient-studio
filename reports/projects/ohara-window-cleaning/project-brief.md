## Client Overview

O'Hara Window Cleaning is a family-run window cleaning business serving South Dublin for over 35 years. Founded by Ciaran O'Hara in 1990, the company offers residential and commercial window cleaning, power washing, gutter clearance, and conservatory valeting. They have a strong reputation with consistently positive reviews on local directories.

Their current website runs WordPress with Divi v4.0.6 (significantly outdated), has no email contact method (phone only), no Google Maps listing, no social media links, and thin content across 3 pages. The site functions as a basic digital brochure but misses key lead generation opportunities.

## Project Goals

Ciaran wants a refreshed website that's easy for customers to find and contact him through. Specific goals from discovery:
- Modern, responsive site with 5-6 pages
- Contact form with email integration (currently phone-only)
- Google Maps / Google Business profile presence
- Keep existing blue and white branding
- No timeline pressure — "whenever it's done"

## Scope Summary

**Included:**
- Responsive WordPress site (5-6 pages): Homepage, Services, Pure Water Washing, About, Contact, + 1 additional
- Contact form with email delivery setup
- Google Business profile creation and optimisation
- Content migration (existing text and photos)
- Basic SEO (meta titles, descriptions, page structure)
- 2 rounds of revisions
- 30-day post-launch support

**Excluded:**
- Professional photography or content writing
- Social media setup or management
- Ongoing SEO or advertising
- E-commerce or online booking
