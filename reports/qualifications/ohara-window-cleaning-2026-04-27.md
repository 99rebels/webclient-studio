## Company Overview

O'Hara Window Cleaning is a family-run Irish window cleaning business operating in South Dublin for over 35 years. Founded by Ciaran O'Hara in 1990, the company serves both residential and commercial clients across a range of services: window cleaning (interior and exterior), power washing, gutter clearance, gutter repairs, conservatory valeting, and post-build clean.

They serve apartments, residential properties, and commercial premises, and also handle specialist work like glue/streak removal from new window installations and post-build debris removal. The business is fully insured and uses a pure water pole-fed system for exterior work and traditional hand cleaning for interiors. They describe their approach as eco-friendly, pet-friendly, and human-friendly.

Based on search results, they have a strong reputation with consistently positive reviews — customers highlight professionalism, punctuality, quality of work, and reasonable pricing. They appear on listings like heydublin.ie ("best window cleaners Dublin"), esda.ie, and have a testimonials page on their own site.

## Fit Assessment

**Score:** 4/10
**Verdict:** MODERATE

**Reasoning:**
- **Need Signal (Low-Medium):** The site functions. It tells visitors what the business does and has a phone number. The Divi version is old and content is thin, but for a window cleaning service, a simple brochure site may be all they feel they need. The lack of email, social links, and Google Business integration suggest the site could work harder for them — but there's no sign they're dissatisfied with it.
- **Size Fit (High):** Family business, 35+ years established, local service area. This is exactly the right size for a freelance web designer. Not enterprise, not too small to afford professional work.
- **Budget Signal (Medium):** 35-year established business in Dublin with a loyal customer base and positive reviews suggests reasonable revenue. However, window cleaning is a trade service — tech/web spending may not be a priority. They invested in WordPress/Divi at some point, so they've paid for web work before, but may see it as a one-and-done expense.
- **Accessibility (Medium):** No email on the site is a friction point for initial outreach — phone is the only contact method. However, for a local Dublin business, a phone call could work. Decision maker is likely Ciaran O'Hara (founder, named on homepage).
- **Timing Signal (Low):** No indication the business is actively looking for web services. The outdated Divi version suggests neglect rather than active dissatisfaction.

## Recommendation

This is a moderate-fit lead. The business is the right size and could clearly benefit from a modernised site with email contact, social links, and Google Business integration. However, there's no active signal they're looking for web services, and the current site — while dated — technically functions. The best angle would be a value-led approach: show them what they're missing (enquiry channel gaps, mobile experience, local SEO) rather than leading with a redesign pitch. A free mini-audit or specific suggestion (e.g., "your site has no email — you're losing enquiries from people who don't want to call") could open a conversation.
