## Executive Summary

O'Hara Window Cleaning is a family-run business serving South Dublin for over 35 years. They currently have a website, but it's outdated (WordPress/Divi v4.0.6) and lacks basic features that potential customers expect — no contact email, no Google Maps listing, and no social media links. During our discovery call, Ciaran expressed interest in a refreshed site that's easy for customers to find and contact him through. This proposal covers a modern, responsive website that addresses these gaps while keeping the blue and white branding the business is known for.

---

## Scope of Work

### Deliverables

- **Responsive Website (5-6 pages)** — Homepage, Services, Pure Water Washing, About, Contact, and one additional page as needed. Built on WordPress with a modern theme. Mobile-first design matching existing blue/white branding.

- **Contact Form with Email Integration** — Replace phone-only contact with a proper contact form that sends to a business email address. Set up the email if needed.

- **Google Business Profile Setup** — Create and optimise a Google Business profile so O'Hara appears in local map searches for 'window cleaner near me' and similar queries.

- **Content Migration** — Transfer existing text content and photos from the current site to the new one. Ciaran has confirmed he has all content and photos available.


### What's included

- Responsive design optimised for mobile, tablet, and desktop

- WordPress CMS with training on how to update content

- Contact form with email delivery setup

- Google Business profile creation and optimisation

- Migration of existing content and photos

- Basic SEO setup (meta titles, descriptions, page structure)

- SSL certificate (HTTPS) if not already active

- 2 rounds of revisions included


### What's NOT included

- Professional photography or content writing

- Social media account setup or management

- Ongoing SEO or advertising campaigns

- Domain registration or hosting costs (discussed separately)

- E-commerce functionality

- Online booking system


### Assumptions

- Client provides all text content and photos within 7 days of project start

- Client provides access to existing hosting and domain registrar

- No custom development beyond standard WordPress functionality

- Design approved before development begins
