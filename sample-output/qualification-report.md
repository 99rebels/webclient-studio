## Company Overview

Summit Dental Care is a family and cosmetic dental practice located in Galway City, Ireland. Established in 2012, the practice serves approximately 3,000 active patients and employs a team of 12 including three dentists, two hygienists, and administrative staff. They offer general dentistry, cosmetic procedures (veneers, whitening, Invisalign), and emergency dental care.

The practice positions itself as a premium provider, with pricing above the local average. They've recently expanded to a second treatment room and are actively looking to grow their patient base in the cosmetic segment.

## Current Web Presence

**Website:** https://www.summitdentalcare.ie
**Platform:** WordPress
**Quality Assessment:** Functional but dated. The site uses a default WordPress theme with minimal customisation. Navigation is cluttered with 14 top-level menu items. The mobile experience is poor — text overlaps on several pages and the booking button is below the fold.

Key observations about their current site:
- Last blog post: March 2024 (over 2 years ago)
- No online booking system — patients must call
- Google Business profile exists but reviews are not displayed on the site
- Page load time: 6.2s on mobile (well above the 3s threshold)
- No SSL certificate on the appointment request form page
- Social media links go to dormant Facebook and Instagram accounts

## Fit Assessment

**Score:** 8/10
**Verdict:** STRONG

**Reasoning:**

- **Size Fit:** High — 12 employees, established practice, actively expanding. Right size for a freelancer project.
- **Budget Signal:** Medium-High — Premium pricing model, recent physical expansion suggests available capital. Invisalign and cosmetic procedures indicate willingness to invest.
- **Accessibility:** High — Practice manager listed on Google Business. Active phone number and email on site.

## Key Findings

- WordPress site on a default theme with no custom branding beyond logo
- Mobile experience is broken — text overlap on treatment pages and contact form
- No online booking integration despite being a service-based business
- Google Business profile has 4.2★ across 87 reviews but is not linked on the website
- SSL certificate missing on the appointment request page — potential security concern for patients
- Last content update was over 2 years ago — SEO impact likely significant
- Competitor practices in Galway have modern, booking-enabled websites

## Recommendation

Strong prospect. The site has clear, visible problems that directly impact patient acquisition. Lead with the mobile experience and online booking gap — these are revenue issues, not cosmetic ones. A modern site with integrated booking could measurably increase new patient inquiries.
